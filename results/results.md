## Treatment effect (intention-to-treat)

* Median survival: D-penicillamine 9.0 years, placebo 9.4 years.
* Log-rank p = 0.750.
* Cox HR for death, D-penicillamine vs placebo: 1.06 (0.75–1.50).

## Competing risks (all 418 patients)

* 10-year cumulative incidence of death without transplant: 52.7%; of transplant: 8.4%.
* Treating transplant as simple censoring and reading 1 − KM would overstate mortality; the
  Aalen–Johansen estimator accounts for the competing event.
